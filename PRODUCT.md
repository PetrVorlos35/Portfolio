# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Three overlapping audiences land on this portfolio: recruiters/hiring managers doing a fast skim (need instant skill match + easy path to CV/LinkedIn/contact), potential freelance clients evaluating trustworthiness and past results before reaching out, and other developers browsing code/craft out of professional curiosity. All three hit the hero first — it has to work as a fast scan AND reward a closer look.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Petr Vorlíček, a fullstack developer based in Prague. Exists to get him hired or contracted: showcase real project work, demonstrate technical + design craft (the site itself is proof of skill), and make it trivial to get in touch. Success = a recruiter/client remembers him and reaches out, or a fellow dev respects the execution.

## Brand Personality

Confident and precise, not showy. "Knows what it's doing" — calm, deliberate, understated. The site should feel like evidence of good judgment (restraint, polish, attention to detail) rather than a shouting portfolio. Personality comes through in craft and small interaction details, not loud color or maximalist layout.

## Anti-references

No specific named anti-reference given. Avoid generic SaaS/AI-portfolio tells regardless: gradient text, tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows, hero-metric templates, identical card grids, side-stripe borders, cream/sand default backgrounds. The site should not read as a template — it should read as considered.

## Design Principles

1. **Restraint as the brand.** Monochrome base + one owned accent color; resist the urge to add more color or ornament to feel "more designed."
2. **Confidence over showmanship.** Motion and detail should feel controlled and intentional, not attention-seeking.
3. **Scan-then-reward.** The hero must work in a 3-second skim (name, role, availability, quick nav) while rewarding attention with small crafted details (hover states, micro-motion, typographic rhythm).
4. **The site is the portfolio piece.** Every interaction is implicitly a demo of Petr's frontend skill — sloppy craft undermines the pitch more than on an ordinary marketing site.
5. **Accessible by default, not as an afterthought.** Contrast and motion accommodations are non-negotiable, not nice-to-haves.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast minimum (≥4.5:1 body text, ≥3:1 large text) — no muted gray text below threshold. Full `prefers-reduced-motion` support: every animation needs a static/instant fallback. Bilingual (cs/en) content via `LanguageContext`.
